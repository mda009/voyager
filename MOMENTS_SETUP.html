On-screen moments

Ten small glass elements on one transparent OBS source (2.20.64–67), fed by what Voyager already knows. Switch each one from Stream deck › On-screen moments or Voyager Control › On-screen moments; the overlay follows within 2 seconds.

ElementWhereWhat it shows
Command hintsTop-leftYour chat commands, one at a time (edit the list on the control page). Hides for 10 minutes after anyone uses a command.
Crew surgeTop-rightWhen 5 supports (follows, subs, gifts, resubs, bits, raids from the Fleet Alerts queue) land within 3 minutes: the count and a 5-minute timer that each new support tops up. A display only: it does not change points.
Dreadnought live HUDRightWhile a fight runs: hull, elapsed time, the Legendary and Hero clocks with the speed bonus, the top five by damage. Boarding countdown in the lobby, result for 20 s after.
Promotion ceremonyCentre-topWhen someone reaches the chosen rank or above (default Commodore): sleeve lace, name, from → to, abbreviation seal, all-time place for the top ranks. 9 s, one at a time.
Next-celebration chipAbove the goals stripHow far the community points are from the next Silver / Gold / Platinum celebration, from the same goal reading the goals strip and Goal FX use. Pulses when close.
Right now tickerBottomThe last 10 events: medals, supports (with the distance to the next celebration), Versus results, promotions, Dreadnought results, plus the live hull while a fight runs. Forgets events after 30 minutes.
Points addedLeft, risingWhenever the Points Ledger reports gains (about every 30 s while Voyager runs): one chip per member with the amount and their new total, largest first, up to 6; the rest are summed into one chip. Gains under the minimum (default 5) are only counted.
Stream streakAcross the upper screenA comet with a trail when a member earns their first points of the day on consecutive stream days (×2 and up): name, streak length, personal best. Up to 5 per scan, the rest summed ("Stream streaks kept · 23 crew · longest ×12").
Giveaway goals stripIts own OBS sourceShows or hides the giveaway goals strip (Voyager_Bucket_Overlay.html, Silver / Gold / Platinum from the Points Ledger). From 2.20.69 the strip has no timer: it flies in when this is on and folds away when off. Place the source in OBS; its box on Screen layout is a reference.
Stream streak · TwitchFrom 2.20.70 Voyager's stream streaks follow Twitch's watch streaks. When a viewer shares their watch streak in chat (Twitch offers it after a few minutes of each stream), Streamer.bot's Watch Streak event reaches Voyager and that number becomes the viewer's streak on the card, in the medals and in the comet (marked "Twitch watch streak"). Between shares Voyager keeps counting on its own: one per stream day (a day counts once 3 viewers earned points), broken by a missed day, which is the same rule as Twitch's "one per stream, broken by a missed stream" unless you stream twice in a day. Needs Streamer.bot 0.2.5 or later for the event; older builds still get the chat message form.
Medal toastBottom-leftOff by default: the medals.html source already shows medal toasts. Turn this on only if you remove that source.

OBS

  1. Add one Browser source to each scene where you want these (or to a nested "Overlays" scene): 1920 × 1080, untick Local file, address http://127.0.0.1:8740/control/Voyager_Moments.html (copy it from the control page). Leave "Shutdown source when not visible" off.
  2. Open Screen layout and drag each moment to where you want it (the next-celebration chip usually just above your goals strip), then Save layout.
  3. Keep Connect chat on: supports, medals, promotions and the goal reading all arrive through Voyager's Streamer.bot connection. The next-celebration chip needs the Voyager - Read OBS Percentages action running (it does when the goals strip is set up).
  4. Press the test buttons on the control page to see each element on stream with the name Sample Pilot.

Screen layout (2.20.66)

Voyager Control › Screen layout shows the whole 1920 × 1080 canvas with every moment held still on it. Drag any box (arrow keys nudge, Shift = 10 px, snap to 4 px) and press Save layout: the overlay moves within 2 seconds. Amber boxes are the other overlays: the Community panel moves for real (its top, right edge and width are saved to the panel), while the dotted ones (camera, goals strip, medal toast, membership card, support alerts, live stats strip, Christmas panel, Dreadnought, Versus) are OBS sources you position in OBS: drag the box to where the source sits, or move the source in OBS to match, and tick the ones you use so overlaps show in red.

Checking

Add ?debug=1 to the source address to see what the overlay last received (which elements are on, how many events, the goal reading). ?preview=1 shows sample data everywhere, which is what the control page's preview uses.

VOYAGER LIVE DASHBOARD — ACTIVE GIVEAWAYS
24 October 2026

INSTALL THIS UPDATE
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open its Voyager_Giveaway folder. Copy the contents into your existing
   Voyager_Giveaway folder, replacing matching files. Keep the rest of your
   existing folder and config.js.
3. Refresh Voyager Control / your OBS Live dashboard dock. Open Live dashboard.
   Active giveaways is near the top, above Live pulse.

If you already installed the shared ticket-panel update immediately before
this update, no further Streamer.bot compilation or OBS changes are needed.
This ZIP also retains that shared-panel update. If you have not installed it,
follow SHARED_TICKET_PANEL_SETUP.txt once to enable its OBS panel switching.

WHAT APPEARS
- Your current tiered/group or impromptu giveaway, including an open group
  preview that has not yet been locked.
- Your independent ticketed campaign running alongside it.
- Giveaway title, included membership groups or participating platforms,
  registered entrants, eligible entrants/tickets and saved draw count.
- Open, paused, locked, drawing, awaiting claim, claim expired, awaiting
  confirmation and winner confirmed statuses where applicable.
- Winner names appear after the 20-second reveal, not during the countdown.
- Ticket closing date/time and time remaining. Times use Australia/Sydney.
- Direct links to the relevant existing controls.
- Completed ticket campaigns appear separately until archived. Archived or
  cleared sessions disappear; older historical draws are not active giveaways.

The existing Giveaway Engine saves one current tiered OR impromptu session.
A combined Silver + Gold + Platinum pool is one giveaway with three groups,
not three separate giveaways. The ticket engine has its own concurrent campaign.
This dashboard lists those saved giveaways; it does not create extra sessions
or turn unused membership groups into active giveaways.

REFRESH AND CONNECTIONS
The section listens for saved-state updates and reads the group/impromptu
engine every 15 seconds. Tickets reuse the Live dashboard's existing ticket
connection and its 30-second refresh. Timer labels update every second.
Refresh status requests a new snapshot from both engines.

Disconnected feeds retain their last received cards and explicitly say
Last known. A missing connection is not reported as an empty giveaway list.
Opening the dashboard, refreshing or reconnecting cannot create a giveaway,
draw/reroll/confirm a winner, change entries, or change panel visibility.
The ordinary engine's existing sync behavior still refreshes open membership
previews; locked pools, saved winners, points and ticket balances are preserved.

No new C# code, action, trigger, point rule or earning rate is required for
the Active giveaways section. Keep these existing actions enabled:
  Voyager - Giveaway Engine
  Voyager - Ticket Giveaway
Keep your working config.js beside the HTML files.

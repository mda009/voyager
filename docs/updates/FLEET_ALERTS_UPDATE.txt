VOYAGER FLEET VISUALS v1.12 - SMOOTHER BATTLE PLAYBACK
24 October 2026

INSTALL INTO YOUR EXISTING VOYAGER FOLDER
1. Close Fleet control and preview pages.
2. Extract this ZIP. Copy the contents of its Voyager_Giveaway folder into
   your existing Voyager_Giveaway folder. Replace the matching files.
   Merge the files; do not delete or replace your entire working folder.
3. In OBS, right-click the Fleet alerts Browser Source, open Properties,
   and click Refresh cache of current page. Keep the same local HTML path
   or URL. Enable Use custom frame rate and set it to 60 FPS.
4. Reopen Fleet controls. Test 04 / Resubscription and 06 / Raid using
   Send test to OBS. In OBS Interact, press D: the build should read 1.12.
5. Close extra preview windows while streaming so OBS does not compete
   with another copy of the 3D scene.

No Streamer.bot import, C# recompile, new trigger or Lumia change is needed.
Your config.js, saved alerts, points and Voyager Versus are not included.
This is an update for an existing Fleet installation, not a replacement
for the entire Voyager Giveaway folder.

WHAT WAS FIXED
- Battle damage lamps previously appeared/disappeared with each hull breach,
  forcing new lighting shaders to compile in the middle of combat. Their
  positions and light effects are retained, but the lamps stay loaded and
  brightness changes as damage appears. The renderer prepares the effect
  variants before reporting graphics ready.
- Gunfire now looks up only shots near the current time. Turret recoil has
  its own per-gun index instead of rescanning thousands of rounds.
- Explosion and cannon noise buffers are generated before weapon playback
  and reused. Audio cues are indexed instead of repeatedly sorting the
  whole combat schedule during each frame.
- Temporary debris transforms and immutable explosion geometry are reused.
- Auto quality starts at up to 1920 pixels wide, retains 4K material maps,
  and applies resolution changes between alerts instead of during battle.
  Native-resolution Ultra remains available through the existing settings.

The hero/escort designs, effects, 20-second resub battle, 28-second raid,
5,549 raid rounds, 204 missile launches, 264 explosion events, transparent
background, captions and raider welcome are retained.

VERIFICATION
Actual WebGL battle/raid frames were checked for shader compilation,
transparent rendering and stage transitions. The tested combat frames
created no new shader programs after loading. 1,190 timestamp comparisons
matched the original gunfire/recoil results, including backwards seeks.
The full raid timeline was checked for missing rounds/impacts and pool
capacity. Noise reuse and deferred resolution changes were checked too.
Tests ran in Chromium with software graphics. Actual frame rate still
varies with OBS settings and the streaming PC's workload.

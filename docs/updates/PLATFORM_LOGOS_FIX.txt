VOYAGER ENTRANT PLATFORM LOGOS 2.1.2 — 25 OCTOBER 2026

INSTALL INTO YOUR EXISTING VOYAGER FOLDER

1. Extract the ZIP. Copy the CONTENTS of its Voyager_Giveaway folder into
   your existing Voyager_Giveaway folder. Replace matching files.
   Keep your existing config.js and the rest of your folder.
2. In OBS, open Properties on your existing giveaway Browser Source and
   click Refresh cache of current page.
3. In Giveaway Control, click Show pool.

Keep the same OBS path: Voyager_Giveaway_Overlay.html. If you use the
standalone Voyager_Entrant_Panel.html source, refresh that source as well.
Do not point OBS at the ZIP or at a new nested copy of the folder.

This is a display-only update for the entrant pool panel. No Streamer.bot
code change or recompilation, new giveaway, or clearing entrants is needed.
Your current giveaway, points, tickets and winner selection are unchanged.

WHAT CHANGED

Each entrant now has a dedicated 24-pixel platform logo in a 30-pixel badge:
purple Twitch, red YouTube with a white play symbol, or green Kick.
The platform name remains underneath the entrant's username. Their profile
picture/initials and membership badges stay separate from the platform logo.

The logos are embedded vectors, so they work without an external image host
and remain sharp when the OBS source is scaled. They remain visible beside
long usernames and if a viewer's profile picture cannot load.

Platform names accept mixed capitalization and surrounding spaces. When
the platform field is missing, the saved platform account key is used.
Unknown accounts are labelled Unknown platform rather than guessed Twitch.

Five visible rows, continuous scrolling, transparency, the Show pool fix,
and the existing wheel / winner / claim sequence are retained.

The small Voyager_Platform_Logos_Fix.zip contains just these five files:
Voyager_Giveaway_Overlay.html
giveaway-sequence-overlay.js
Voyager_Entrant_Panel.html
giveaway-pool-panel.js
giveaway-pool-panel.css

The same fix is included in the updated full Sequence and Ticket packages.
Install only one package; the small fix is sufficient for this update.

Verified in a local browser with all three platforms, open and locked pools,
member previews, scrolling, long names, missing profile pictures, refresh,
and the countdown / wheel / claim handoff. Live OBS must be refreshed on
your computer to load the new files.

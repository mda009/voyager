VOYAGER REPORTING - AVAILABLE DATA PANELS

This replaces the empty Viewer countries, Devices and Discovery sources panels
on the Reporting page with:

1. Viewing time - hours watched, daily bars, hours streamed and average viewers
   where supplied. If watched time is absent, it shows hours streamed instead.
2. Follower growth - reported follows, daily bars and best reported day.
3. Subscriptions - reported total with paid, Prime and gifted categories where
   supplied. If the total is missing, the headline names the supplied category.

INSTALL THIS REPORTING CHANGE

1. Close the Voyager Command Center page/dock.
2. Extract this ZIP. Copy the files inside Voyager_Giveaway into your existing
   Voyager_Giveaway folder, accepting replacement of matching files.
   Merge into the folder; do not delete your existing folder first.
3. Reopen Voyager_Command_Center.html?page=reporting, or use Reporting in the
   Voyager navigation. Refresh the OBS dock/browser if an old page remains.

No new Streamer.bot action or C# compilation is needed for this reporting change.
No CSV re-upload is needed when your reports are already saved in this browser.
Use the existing page location and browser/OBS profile to retain saved reports.

The seven files required specifically for this change are:

  stream-pages.js

HOW THE FIGURES WORK

- Uses the existing Previous 30 days (default), Previous 14 days, Previous 7 days,
  Latest stream and individual saved report controls.
- Refreshes after report imports, saved edits and reporting-window changes.
- Imported figures are historical. These panels do not fetch new Twitch CSVs.
- Rolling windows end yesterday in the source report's time zone.
- Exact whole-period source totals take precedence over daily sums. A missing
  metric may use available daily reports; coverage is shown per metric.
- Daily dates are deduplicated using the existing reporting rules. Source,
  time zone and currency sets stay separate.
- Missing dates are crosses, not zero. A dot indicates an explicitly reported
  zero. Signed follower and subscription adjustments retain their signs.
- Minutes are converted to hours per date. Hours and minutes are not counted
  twice. Viewer-hours are not estimated from averages.
- Subscription categories may overlap, so they are not added to invent a total.
- Large individual periods chart their last 60 dates; totals cover the selection.
- Latest stream follows the existing daily-report logic; it cannot split multiple
  streams within one exported date.
- Existing manual country/device/discovery data is retained in saved reports,
  the manual editor and exports.

Your config.js, twitch-analytics-data.js and saved points/ticket/giveaway data
are not included or replaced by this update.

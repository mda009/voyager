VOYAGER VERSUS v1.8 — STANDALONE UPDATE

New live battles: global cooldown 45 minutes; winner +50 points; runner-up 0.
The challenger can join an open call immediately. No zero-point payment is queued.

1. Stop the old launcher (Ctrl+C in its console).
2. Extract the new package. Copy its program files into your existing game
   folder, accepting replacement. KEEP local/data, rankings.json, economy.json
   and your saved connection settings. Do not delete the working folder.
3. In Streamer.bot, open the existing Voyager Versus Rewards v1.5 action.
   Replace its entire Execute C# Code with dist/Voyager-Versus-Rewards.cs.
   Click Compile, save, and rename the action exactly:
      Voyager Versus Rewards v1.8
   Keep it enabled on a dedicated blocking queue, with no chat trigger.
4. Restart START-VOYAGER.bat (or sh start-voyager.sh on macOS/Linux).
5. Refresh the battle and cooldown OBS Browser Source caches and your control
   page. Their URLs are unchanged. The setup panel should show 50 / 0 and
   the timer should use 45 minutes from the original live call.

An existing 30-minute cooldown extends to 45 minutes from its original call,
exactly once. Restarting does not start another 45-minute period. Previously
earned pending rewards retain their recorded amounts; new battles use 50 / 0.
The updated action also announces the new rewards when !versus becomes ready.
Do not delete payment receipts or economy.json to retry a reward.

For Voyager Control installation, use VERSUS_RULES_UPDATE.txt in the main
Voyager_Giveaway folder instead. Full standalone setup: dist/START-HERE.html.

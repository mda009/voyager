VOYAGER — ONE OBS PANEL FOR LEADERBOARDS AND TICKET GIVEAWAYS
Shared panel update 1.0 / panel action 1.3 — 24 October 2026

WHAT CHANGES
Show ticket panel replaces the community leaderboard inside your existing
Voyager_Leaderboard_Panel.html Browser Source. Its saved width, top/right
position, opacity and scroll speed carry across. Five rows remain visible;
all eligible ticket accounts are scrolled, not just the top ten.

On Live dashboard, choose Watch time, Subscription months, Voyager points,
Pilots or Gifted subs and press Show in OBS to switch back. There is only
one saved selection, shared by your browser, dock and OBS source.

INSTALL
1. Extract this ZIP to a temporary folder. Open its Voyager_Giveaway folder.
   Copy the files inside into your EXISTING Voyager_Giveaway folder and
   replace matching files. Do not delete your existing folder.
   Keep config.js and all other existing files in place.

2. In Streamer.bot, open Actions and find your EXISTING action named:
       Voyager - Leaderboard Panel
   Open its Execute C# Code sub-action. Open the supplied
   Voyager_Leaderboard_Panel.cs in Notepad, select all and copy.
   Replace the code in that existing sub-action, then Compile and Save.
   Keep its existing references and queue settings. This adds no new
   assemblies, triggers or actions. Do not import a duplicate panel action.
   Do NOT replace the code in Voyager - Ticket Giveaway or any points action.

3. In OBS, open Properties for the Browser Source currently displaying your
   watch-time/community leaderboard. Its file/URL should end in:
       Voyager_Leaderboard_Panel.html
   Keep that source and its current scene transform. Click Refresh cache of
   current page. Keep the source enabled; Voyager controls its contents.
   Its normal browser canvas is 1920 x 1080; retain your existing setup.

4. Remove the old separate Voyager_Ticket_Overlay.html source from this scene.
   If you prefer to keep it as a backup, enable its "Shutdown source when
   not visible" option and turn its eye OFF. This also stops the old source
   from playing duplicate ticket winner audio in the background.
   Do not remove Voyager_Leaderboard_Panel.html or your ordinary giveaway,
   wheel, fleet, points or member overlay sources.

5. Refresh your Live dashboard and the Ticketed giveaway control page/dock.
   Click Show ticket panel. Your ticket giveaway should now occupy exactly
   the position of the previous leaderboard. Pick a community leaderboard
   and click Show in OBS to switch back.

USING IT
- Show ticket panel works while collection is open, paused or locked.
- Hide ticket panel only hides the shared source when tickets are selected.
  It cannot accidentally hide a leaderboard selected from another dock.
- Community leaderboard Hide panel hides whichever selection occupies the slot.
- Panel settings & preview now also offers Ticketed giveaway. The saved
  position, size, speed, opacity and auto-hide duration apply to this slot.
  Ticket title, entrants and balances come directly from the active campaign.
  Ticket display always includes its participating platforms; the leaderboard
  top-list limit, custom title and exclusions do not filter the ticket pool.
- Draw/reroll/confirm in Ticket controls use the existing reveal/congratulations
  animation in this SAME Browser Source. Only Streamer.bot selects a winner.
  Showing the panel again returns to the list. Reloading OBS does not draw again.
- No new giveaway is needed. Tickets, watch time, eligibility, manual awards,
  points, membership, platform account identities and saved histories are
  unchanged. Ticket collection continues even while a leaderboard is showing.
- Impromptu and tiered giveaway engines and their existing sources are unchanged.

IF TICKETS WILL NOT SHOW
- "Choose a leaderboard" means the original panel C# is still running:
  complete step 2 in the existing Voyager - Leaderboard Panel action.
- If the previous layout remains, refresh BOTH the OBS Browser Source cache
  and the control dock/page after copying all included files.
- If two panels or duplicate reveal audio appear, check for another active
  Voyager_Ticket_Overlay.html source, including nested scenes.
- Use the existing config.js with your working Streamer.bot WebSocket settings.

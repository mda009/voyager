Four weeks of loyalty.

A separate ticketed giveaway runs alongside your existing tiered or impromptu giveaway. Viewers join once, earn one ticket for every ten tracked live minutes, and keep their tickets across streams. You can add or deduct tickets and draw a winner weighted by ticket balance.

Watchtime or tickets showing zero? Start here

Entry and earning are separate. A chat command registers the account. The ticket action must also receive that platform's Present Viewers event while live. The previous WATCHTIME column counted earned tickets. v1.3 replaces it with Tracked time and a progress bar showing the saved partial minutes until the next ticket.

  1. Copy the update files into your existing folder, keeping config.js. On your existing Voyager - Ticket Giveaway → Execute C# Code, replace all code with Voyager_Ticket_Giveaway.cs, then Compile and save. Keep one enabled action with Concurrent off on an unpaused blocking queue.
  2. On that same action, attach and enable all three triggers: Twitch → General → Present Viewers, YouTube → General → Present Viewers, Kick → General → Present Viewers. Keep the chat triggers for entry. Presence triggers attached only to your points or community actions do not execute this ticket action.
  3. For the simplest setup, set Platforms → Twitch → Settings → Present Viewers to Live Update on and 1 minute. In ticket control's Tracking Health card, leave Twitch update interval at 1 minute. If you prefer Twitch updates every five or ten minutes, choose that same interval in both places and click Save tracking interval in Voyager. This does not remotely change Streamer.bot settings.
  4. Keep the campaign Open, each platform enabled, Kick connected and your live YouTube broadcast monitored. YouTube and Kick emit presence events every minute; their activity-window slider is not a sample interval. Their account lists depend on recent chat activity, so a silent viewer may fall out of the presence list.
  5. Reload the ticket page. In Tracking Health, confirm recent events, valid accounts and registered entrants. The first valid event sets a baseline. A registered account present in the next consecutive event begins accumulating tracked time. After ten credited minutes, one ticket is added automatically. Use real live presence; simulated Test Trigger events do not award time.

What “watchtime” means here: Streamer.bot tracks identifiable chat presence, not video playback. Twitch Live Update uses its live chatter list. YouTube and Kick use recent chat activity, so silent viewers can be missed and recently active users can remain present for the configured activity window. Anonymous viewers cannot earn account tickets. This is a watchtime estimate, not proof that a viewer is watching. Accounts on different platforms are kept separate; they are not automatically merged into one person.

No new Streamer.bot commands need to be created: the native chat triggers recognise the commands below. Existing Command Triggered routes are also supported when the command source is Twitch, YouTube or Kick. If Tom's bot is still responding to !entergiveaway or !tickets, disable its matching commands before using those names for Voyager, or use Voyager's aliases while testing.

4. Create your four-week giveaway

  1. Click Create ticketed giveaway, enter its title, and choose the enabled platforms. Twitch, YouTube and Kick are selected by default; deselect platforms you do not use. The deadline defaults to 28 days from now, in your computer's local time. You can change the deadline before creation.
  2. Enter bot/broadcaster logins to exclude, separated by commas. A plain login applies across platforms; twitch:login limits an exclusion to one platform.
  3. Leave Announce the giveaway selected to submit its title and entry instructions to the selected platform chats. YouTube's creation announcement requires exactly one monitored broadcast. Chat delivery failures are logged by Streamer.bot; ticket state remains saved.
  4. Click Create & open entries. Viewers type !entergiveaway once to join. They begin with zero tickets and receive a registration message on their originating platform.
CHAT COMMANDRESULT

!ticketgiveawayShow the current campaign title, status and earning rate.
!ticket / !enterContinue to work with your existing impromptu giveaway. They do not join the ticket campaign.

Replies are limited to one per account every 15 seconds. There is no chat message for every automatic ticket award. The control page and OBS panel update with each presence sample.

How the timer earns tickets

  • One ticket requires 600,000 milliseconds of tracked live presence: ten minutes earns one ticket; an hour earns six. The first presence sample establishes a baseline. No ticket is granted just for joining.
  • An account must be present in consecutive samples, after it joined, while collection is open and that platform is live. Partial minutes carry between streams: seven minutes today plus three tomorrow earns one ticket.
  • Pausing, locking, restarting Streamer.bot, stream-boundary resets, or a sample gap longer than the expected interval plus 30 seconds starts a new presence baseline. Twitch accepts the selected 1–10 minute cadence; YouTube and Kick use one minute. The system does not backfill unknown time. Already earned tickets and partial minutes remain saved.
  • Collection is cut off at the deadline. The status becomes locked when Streamer.bot next handles a sample, chat command or state refresh. Closed/offline dashboards do not extend earning past the deadline. The final partial sampling interval can be omitted if a lock or refresh happens before its next presence sample.
  • Leave Streamer.bot running for earning. You can close the browser and OBS panel without resetting the giveaway. No Voyager Versus host or external ticket service is needed.

5. Allocate or deduct tickets manually

  1. Under Manual allocation, select an account. Registered entrants and known live-presence accounts appear in the list.
  2. Enter a whole number: 10 adds ten tickets; -3 deducts three. Enter a reason and review the displayed before/after balance, then click Apply adjustment. A balance cannot go below zero.
  3. For an offline Twitch account, expand Find an offline Twitch account, look up its exact login, then allocate tickets. YouTube and Kick accounts must first appear in presence data or join through chat so Voyager knows their stable platform ID.
  4. Use Exclude account or Restore eligibility with a reason. Excluded accounts keep their ledger but cannot earn or win. Manual allocation registers a new known account; restoring it can override a configured exclusion.

Manual changes are recorded with time, account, amount and reason. Watchtime and manual tickets are separate columns. You can allocate or correct tickets while open, paused or locked, until the first draw. The first draw freezes balances and eligibility for that campaign.

6. Add the independent OBS panel

  1. In OBS, add a new Browser Source, for example Voyager Tickets. Check Local file and select Voyager_Ticket_Overlay.html from your installed folder.
  2. Set its browser dimensions to 1920 × 1080. Fit the source to your canvas; it also scales cleanly on a 4K canvas. Keep the background transparent. Use the same folder/config as the control page.
  3. The compact panel sits at the left, with five rows and continuous scrolling. Your existing giveaway panel can stay on the right. The panel shows the title, status, account platform, ticket balance, total eligible tickets and entry command.
  4. Use Show ticket panel or Hide ticket overlay from ticket control. These buttons affect this source only. Refresh its browser cache after copying future updates.

The dashboard contains a muted preview. Its sample mode is labelled Sample data; use the ordinary file without ?demo=1 in OBS.

7. Draw, confirm and archive

  1. At the deadline, or whenever you choose, Lock entries. Locking stops new entries and watchtime collection; it does not draw automatically. Before the deadline and before any draw, you can reopen collection.
  2. Click Draw winner. Every eligible ticket has one chance. For example, ten tickets in a pool of 100 gives that account a 10% chance.
  3. The new OBS source plays your existing GiveawayOS_Reveal.html winner animation. Wait for its 20-second reveal, then Confirm winner to play Voyager_Congratulations.html. You can instead reroll an unconfirmed winner.
  4. All previously selected accounts, including rerolled winners, are excluded from subsequent picks. Use Draw next winner for another prize after confirming. Each draw saves its eligible ticket weights and winning ticket before the result is shown.
  5. Click Complete giveaway, then Archive & prepare next. The complete ledger and draw history remain saved; the next ticket campaign starts empty.

Your tiered/impromptu giveaway keeps its existing controls and OBS source. Both systems can collect and remain visible concurrently. For a clear on-screen result, run their winner animations at different times.

8. Backups, exports and checks

  • Export tickets CSV exports all accounts and balances. Export full ledger saves the active state and its draw/audit history. The archive selector exports a completed giveaway's full JSON ledger.
  • Tickets persist in Streamer.bot's persisted global VoyagerTicketGiveawayV1; archived ledgers use VoyagerTicketArchive_…. Back up your Streamer.bot data during the four-week campaign. Copying the HTML folder alone does not back up balances. Exported JSON is for review/backup; this version has no automatic JSON re-import button.
  • If the connection times out, refresh and inspect the saved balance or draw before trying again. Reconnection reads state and never automatically repeats allocations or draws. A paused/busy Voyager Tickets queue can prevent confirmations.
  • To check live earning: create a clearly named test campaign, join with a connected account, verify Tracking Health receives one-minute samples, then allow ten tracked minutes after the first baseline sample. Complete and archive this test before creating the real campaign. Streamer.bot's simulated isTest events deliberately do not award tickets.
  • This update is tested with a controlled CPH adapter and browser checks; see TICKET_GIVEAWAY_UPDATE.txt for the validation performed. It has not been run on your Windows PC or connected to your real platform accounts. If compilation fails, copy the error text and your Streamer.bot version.

No currency changes: giveaway tickets are their own ledger. This feature does not award, deduct, migrate or reset Voyager points, Twitch channel points, memberships, or existing giveaway entrants. Manual allocations are capped at ±100,000 per operation. The campaign supports up to 20,000 registered accounts and one billion total tickets.

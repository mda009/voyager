MDA009 / VOYAGER CONTROL

1 point every 10 minutes.

This update changes the watchtime earning rate to 1 Voyager point per detected viewer every 10 minutes while live. The older Currency Core and StreamUP Present Viewers actions now share the same timing check. Running both cannot double the watchtime award.

Existing installation: use the small two-action update below.

No OBS files, web settings, new triggers or new timers are required. Your current balances, groups, giveaway buckets and historical records are retained. Chat, subscription, bits, referral, streak and Versus rewards keep their existing rules.

Install in Streamer.bot

  1. Make a backup of your current Streamer.bot actions. Briefly disable [Currency Core] - Timed Action and Currency System • Events while applying the update.
  2. Click Import and drag in Voyager_Watchtime_10_Minute_Update.sb. It should list exactly Currency System • Events and [Currency Core] - #Code. Import the replacements for those existing actions.
  3. Open Currency System • Events → Execute C# Code. Keep its Name exactly Currency System • Events. Click Compile, then Save. Keep its current StreamUP and Newtonsoft references.
  4. Open [Currency Core] - #Code → No Touchy/Definitely No Touchy → Execute C# Code (the group title can differ). Keep its Name [Currency Core]. Click Compile, then Save.
  5. After both compile successfully, enable Currency System • Events and re-enable [Currency Core] - Timed Action if it was enabled before. Keep your existing Present Viewers triggers and update interval.

The patch uses the exact action and sub-action IDs from your supplied setup. If you have made separate custom edits to these two C# actions, merge only the watchtime changes instead of overwriting those edits. Full replacement sources are included as Voyager_Currency_Events.cs and Voyager_Currency_Core.cs.

Awarding happens on the first Present Viewers check at or after each 10-minute period. If checks arrive late, the award arrives late; there are no catch-up payouts. The first check starts a new period. A viewer missing from a snapshot starts again on return. Offline checks, missing viewer data, or a gap longer than 11 minutes reset the period. Restarting Streamer.bot also starts a fresh period, without crediting downtime.

Each platform account is tracked independently by its user ID. The supported paths are Twitch, YouTube and Kick, where their existing Present Viewers triggers are enabled. Streamer.bot must detect the account as present; this cannot measure anonymous or otherwise undetected viewers. It is based on detected presence, not verified video playback.

Watchtime always adds exactly 1 to the persisted points field used by your Voyager ledger. Subscriber/group multipliers do not increase this award. StreamUP's enabled excluded-user group is respected; excluded users are skipped. Existing StreamUP Present Viewers reward amounts are superseded by this fixed watchtime rate.

Check the next award

  1. During a live stream, leave a known account detected as present. Its first detected check starts the timer.
  2. After 10 minutes and the next viewer check, open Voyager → Points & membership → member history. There should be one +1 watchtime award.
  3. With your existing movement listeners working, the entry is labelled Watchtime / 1 point per 10 minutes. Older unclassified entries are retained as they were. Other rewards can still increase the balance between watchtime awards.

Using Test Trigger does not award points. If the update reports an error, leave the two award actions disabled until both C# actions compile successfully. To undo this specific update, restore those two actions from your backup; that restores their prior earning rate.

Validation: the included C# is checked with a local Streamer.bot/StreamUP API test harness. Compile on your streaming PC to confirm compatibility with your installed DLLs. No live points were changed while preparing this download.

Goal FX 2.0: giveaway goal met

When Silver, Gold or Platinum community points cross their threshold, the full-screen Voyager Goal FX source celebrates it in the glass design: that tier's tile lifts off the goals strip, flies to the centre and opens into the tier emblem, GOAL REACHED in the tier metal, shock rings, sparks and confetti, then folds back onto the strip. About 10 seconds. No sound comes from this source; your Streamer.bot actions play the audio as before.

Upgrading from Goal FX v1.3

  1. OBS: open the properties of your existing Voyager Goal FX browser source and point its local file at Voyager_Goal_FX.html in your Voyager folder (1920 × 1080). Keep the source name exactly Voyager Goal FX: the Streamer.bot reader checks it is in Program. Refresh the cache.
  2. Streamer.bot: open the action Voyager - Read OBS Percentages › its Execute C# Code, replace the code with goal-fx/Voyager_OBS_Bridge.cs from your Voyager folder, click Compile, then Save and Compile. The only change is the Platinum target, now 200,000 (it was 220,000), matching the Points Ledger and the goals strip. Keep its 2-second timer and the Hold/Release steps in your Upgrade Animation groups: the new overlay honours the same upgrade hold.
  3. Right-click the source › Interact, press D: the panel should say connected · subscribed and show fresh readings. Test Gold plays the celebration without changing points. Press D or Escape to hide the panel before going live.

Where the animation starts from

The tile lifts off from where your goals strip sits on the scene. Tell the source where that is with ?from=x,y,spread on the file address: the centre of the Gold tile in 1920 × 1080 scene coordinates and the distance between tile centres. The default, ?from=960,62,300, suits the strip across the top of the scene at 960 × 120. For a strip at the bottom use something like ?from=960,1020,300.

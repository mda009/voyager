SETTINGS

How they behave

Points added plays when the Points Ledger reports gains (every 30 s or so while Voyager runs): one chip per member, largest first, the rest summed into one chip. Stream streak plays when a member earns their first points of the day on consecutive stream days. The crew surge is a display: it shows the pile-up and a timer and does not change points by itself. The medal toast is off by default because the medals.html source already shows one; turn it on here only if you remove that source.

VOYAGER 2.18

Voyager Medals setup

Medals reward the viewers who have been with the crew the longest and support it the most, plus everyone who takes part. Every medal has four grades: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Legend. Medals are prestige only: they never pay points or giveaway tickets and never change anyone's odds. Once earned, a medal is never taken away.

What you need

  • Voyager 2.18 or later, running, with Streamer.bot connected (Voyager Versus › Stream setup).
  • Voyager - Community Tracker (watch time, subs and gifts) and Voyager - Fleet Alerts (Bits, Kicks, Super Chats, gift drops), which you already use for Missions and Support alerts.
  • One new Streamer.bot action, Voyager - Medals, below.

1. Create the action

  1. In Streamer.bot, Actions › right-click › Add. Name it exactly Voyager - Medals. Untick Concurrent.
  2. Add a sub-action Core › C# › Execute C# Code. Delete the sample code, paste the whole of Voyager_Medals.cs from your Voyager folder, click Compile and Save and Compile.
  3. The action only reads Community Tracker, Fleet Alerts and giveaway winners. It never changes points, tickets, groups or giveaways.

2. Add the !medals command

  1. Commands › right-click › Add: !medals, Permitted: Everyone. Tick Twitch, YouTube and Kick sources. Voyager limits replies to one every 30 seconds per viewer.
  2. On the action, Triggers › right-click › Core › Commands › Command Triggered › !medals.
  3. Viewers get a reply like: @nova 🏅 12 medals (3 Gold, 1 Legend) · ★ 46 honour, #7 in the Hall of Honour · latest: Time Watched Gold · closest next: Fleet Gifter Silver (3 gifted subs to go).

3. Add it to OBS

  1. Add a Browser source, 1920 × 1080, address http://127.0.0.1:8740/medals.html (add ?side=right for the right side). Leave "Local file" unticked.
  2. It is invisible until someone earns a medal. By default Silver and above show on stream; every medal is announced in chat (grouped, at most one line every 20 seconds). Change both in Voyager Control › Medals.
  3. Click Preview unlock on stream on that page to check the source.

4. First run

Voyager reads everyone's history about a minute after it connects and awards those medals quietly, with one summary line in chat, so chat isn't flooded. After that, new medals are announced as they're earned.

Honour: Bronze 1 ★, Silver 2 ★, Gold 4 ★, Legend 8 ★, specials 4 ★. Medals in the three ×2 groups count double, so the longest-serving viewers and biggest supporters rank highest in the Hall of Honour.

Good to know

  • Counted from launch: stream streaks, sub streaks, Versus win streaks, gift drops, Super Chats, giveaway wins and night streams are tracked from the day you install 2.18. Watch time, months subscribed, gifted subs, Bits, Kicks, Voyager points and Versus wins include history.
  • Follower Since uses the earliest date Voyager knows for a viewer: their first Community Tracker or Fleet Alerts record. Platforms don't share old follow dates, and YouTube keeps channel subscriptions private.
  • Stream days are days when at least three viewers earned Voyager points, so an off-stream gift doesn't break anyone's streak.
  • Points data (Point Collector, stream days and streaks) comes from the Points Ledger, sent while Voyager Control or the Public website page is open.
  • All grade targets can be changed, and any medal switched off, in Voyager Control › Medals.

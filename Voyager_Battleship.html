ON STREAM

The battle

—
Press Start a battle. The splash appears and chat has 30 seconds to join with !fire.
!fire B5Free. Shoots that square: a letter for the column, a number for the row (typing just B5 works too). One shot every 6 seconds per person; a square that is already shot costs nothing. Only shots earn tickets, and only a shot can land the final hit.
CheersBits and Super Chats (100 or more) ping the sonar: one hidden ship square is marked as a contact for every 100, up to 3 a cheer. A contact is not a hit; someone still has to shoot it.
Gift subsEach gifted sub calls an air strike on a random unshot square (up to 5 a gift). An air strike never lands the final hit.

Add it in OBS once: Browser source, 1920 × 1080 (any 16:9 size works), at this address. It is invisible until you start a battle, and viewers cannot start one. The on-screen display follows the Dreadnought: lobby splash, nameplate and bar, clock, gun-crew panel with the MVP, the enemy fleet roster, feed, banners and the result card.

Stage takeover (2.38): if the game stage source is in OBS (Dreadnought › Stage takeover), a battle plays there exactly like the Dreadnought: the splash slams in, your scene shrinks to the corner, the panels arrive around the game, and the MVP is celebrated at the end. This source then steps aside by itself. Switch it per game on the Stage takeover tab.

If time runs out, nobody is paid.

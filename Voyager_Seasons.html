GOALS IN THIS RUN

Goals

Each goal has its own target, points reward and (optionally) its own due date. When a goal is reached, everyone who helped (at least the minimum) earns its reward — pay it from Missions › Points owed. Goals can count subs and gifts, Voyager points earned by the crew, or Voyager Versus wins — or track Twitch active subscribers (live count): the goal shows Twitch's own subscriber number (read through Voyager - Stream Stats' Get Subscriber Count every 5 minutes, and whenever a dashboard asks), so it moves with your real sub count rather than adding up events. Everyone who subscribed or renewed on Twitch this run counts as a helper for the reward.

RUN A COMMUNITY GOAL

No community goal running

Nothing runs on its own. Set the goals below, give the community goal a name and a due date, then press Start. Standings and every goal count from that moment; it ends on its due date (or when you end it) and the top five go into the Hall of Fame. The next one starts only when you start it.

GOALS

Community goals

Each goal has its own target, points reward and (optionally) its own due date. When a goal is reached, everyone who helped (at least the minimum) earns its reward — pay it from Missions › Points owed. Goals can count subs and gifts, Voyager points earned by the crew, or Voyager Versus wins.

STANDINGS

Community goal score

Score = points earned during the community goal + the supporter bonus for every sub, renewal or gifted sub. Standings are separate from points balances: spending points never lowers them, and nobody loses saved points when a community goal ends.

OBS · FESTIVE PANEL

Christmas goal panel

A dedicated on-stream panel for the running community goal: countdown to the due date, every goal with its progress, reward and helpers, in the glass card design with a festive finish and gentle snow when the name says Christmas. It appears by itself when a community goal is running and hides when none is.

ON STREAM

The hunt

—
Press Start a hunt. A wanted splash appears and chat has 30 seconds to join with !track.
!trackFree. Joins the hunt, then tightens the search ring each time it counts (once every 8 seconds per person). Only !track earns tickets, and only a !track can land the final fix.
CheersBits and Super Chats (100 or more) fire a scanner pulse that tightens the ring faster, capped per cheer. It helps the whole crew.
Gift subsEach gifted sub launches a wingman that keeps tightening the ring by itself until the hunt ends.

Add it in OBS once: Browser source, 1920 × 1080 (any 16:9 size works), at this address. It is invisible until you start a hunt, and viewers cannot start one. The on-screen display follows the Dreadnought: lobby splash, nameplate and bar, clock, hunter panel with the MVP, feed, banners and the result card.

Stage takeover (2.38): if the game stage source is in OBS (Dreadnought › Stage takeover), a hunt plays there exactly like the Dreadnought: the splash slams in, your scene shrinks to the corner, the panels arrive around the game, and the MVP is celebrated at the end. This source then steps aside by itself. Switch it per game on the Stage takeover tab.

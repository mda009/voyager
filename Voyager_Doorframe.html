    SETTINGS

    How a round runs

    The most-typed command decides. A tie, or nobody typing, counts as a bonk. Points go to everyone who typed the right command for a door, even when chat as a whole got it wrong, and are paid automatically like other Voyager points.

    IN THE WAY

    Obstacles

    0

    A new one at every door; each comes up once before any comes round again. !hop over floor things, !duck under hanging things, !squeeze past things beside the door.

    YOUR OWN

    Your own obstacles appear on screen as a mystery crate with their name on the sign, and use your punchline when she hits them.

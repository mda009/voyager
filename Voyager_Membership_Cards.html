WHAT CHAT TYPES
!voyager

Their card: tier, points, leaderboard, time watched, Versus, season, Dreadnought, months subscribed, Honour, six medals and progress to the next tier.

!voyager stats

The back of the card: Dreadnought, Versus, missions and seasons, watch streak, goals helped and giveaways won.

!voyager badges

The medal wall: earned medals in their grade, locked ones with progress.

!voyager title <medal>

Choose the callsign under their name from medals they've earned. !voyager private hides time watched and months subscribed (!voyager public shows them again).

Tiers and upgrades work exactly as before: your BLUE/SILVER/GOLD/PLATINUM groups and !upgrade. When someone is added to Silver, Gold or Platinum, the card plays the tier-up ceremony. Giveaway tickets are never shown.

SETTINGS

Cards

Prices only drive the card's next-tier bar. They match your Streamer.bot purchase actions (1,200 / 1,800 / 17,000); if you change those, change these too.

AFTER FLEET ALERTS

Cards after follows & support

Cards wait for the Fleet animation (and any alerts queued right behind it) to finish, then play on the same card source. Points here only drive the "+points" on the card: set them to what your Streamer.bot currency pays, or 0 to leave points out. Months, gifts and medals come from what the alert says.

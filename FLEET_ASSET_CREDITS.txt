https://robertsspaceindustries.com/media/l8fs5ivw36mdvr/source/RSI-Polaris.ctm
Original model SHA-256: 7f03de5a897f4fde4f6385cf89c702cca4107638da55759689e4283333310ce5

The source is concept-era holoviewer geometry. It is not the current in-game
Polaris asset or the official high-resolution game texture set.
Derived for this overlay: duplicate/collapsed triangle removal, welded topology, real-time mesh
simplification, normal reconstruction, spatial assembly sections, normalised
orientation, vertex colours and projected UV coordinates.
Hero: 519,842 triangles / 259,882 quantised source vertices.
Hero assembly wireframe LOD: 61,496 triangles / 29,931 quantised source vertices.
Vertices are expanded/recomputed for creased normals at runtime.

MATERIALS AND EFFECTS
New original Voyager v1.3 material maps generated from a coherent armour layout:
4096 x 4096 base colour, normal, and packed AO/roughness/metalness WebP maps.
The packed map supplies cavity AO (red), roughness (green) and metalness (blue).
Colour is sRGB; normal and packed material data are linear.
Original added geometry: 40 surface fittings and three guided VX-09 torpedoes.
These added fittings/weapons are Voyager designs, not extracted game assets.
Textures are embedded in fleet-assets/surface-data.js for local OBS use.
Hull paint, projected mapping, dock, engines, sparks, guided weapons, 3D impact shaders,
shield and camera choreography are Voyager additions. Audio is synthesised
locally from oscillator/noise effects; no third-party sound recording is used.

BUILD / RUNTIME
fleet-renderer.mjs, fleet-hull-details.mjs and fleet-ordnance.mjs are editable
renderer sources; fleet-assets/hull-anchors.mjs stores baked attachment points. fleet-assets/fleet-renderer.js
is its browser-ready IIFE bundle; OBS loads that bundle directly. Rebuilding the
source requires the full Studio project's Voyager_Versus/dist/vendor modules
and an esbuild installation. The delivered overlay does not require Node/npm,
a web server, a CDN, a bundler or internet access for its visual assets.
The Streamer.bot WebSocket server is required for live event playback.

VARIED FLEET AND WORKING SHIPYARD — v1.7
Original Voyager vessel models: corvette, frigate, carrier, lancer, tender;
siege ship, raider, marauder, destroyer, torpedo ship; needle, scout,
interceptor, bomber, dart and raptor. These are original procedural designs,
not official Star Citizen ship models. Each class has distinct geometry,
engine layouts and fittings; material changes alone do not define a class.
New original environments: two travelling gantry cranes with winches/cables,
four articulated welders, service tugs, six release clamps, launch machinery,
shield charging pods, animated base shutters, radar, pipework and radiators.
New PulseBattery model: time-based finite-speed rounds, muzzle flashes,
recoil, local light, impact flashes and hot fragments; generated cannon audio.
Editable sources additionally include fleet-vessels.mjs, fleet-environments.mjs,
fleet-gunfire.mjs and fleet-raid.mjs. All are compiled into the local IIFE.

HOW CHAT PLAYS
!join then !fire

Free for everyone. Lasers deal 12–22 damage, once every 4 seconds. 1 in 16 shots is a critical hit (×3).

Cheers

Bits and YouTube Super Chats fire missiles: 2.5 damage per bit (1.00 of Super Chat counts as 100 bits).

Gifted subs

Twitch gift subs, YouTube gifted memberships and Kick gifted subs fire torpedoes: 600 damage each.

Chat has to !join to take part (cheering or gifting joins automatically). At 50% hull the boss raises shields for 8 seconds (!fire is halved); at 20% it is enraged. Every 15 seconds it knocks a few pilots out for 10 seconds. Victory pays the same points to every pilot who fought; if it escapes, nobody gets points, paid from Missions › Points owed. Support only changes damage, never rewards.

SETTINGS

The fight

Speed goals (2.20.44): a kill within the Legendary time earns every pilot who fought the Legendary Strike medal; within the Hero time, Hero of the Fleet (a Legendary kill counts for both). Either adds the bonus to each pilot's victory points (100 + 10% = 110). The overlay shows the time left to each goal during the fight. Set a time to 0 to turn that goal off.

The raffle

—
Press Start a raffle. It opens for the minutes you set; everyone who types !join gets one ticket with a random number.

Two OBS Browser sources, added once. Both are invisible until you start a raffle, and viewers cannot start one.

Animations · 1920 × 1080
Panel · 460 × 640

The animations source plays the opening ticket, a "you are in" stub for each new entry and the draw. The panel shows the time left to join, how many are in and the newest tickets, then the winners; it leaves 30 seconds after the raffle ends.

    SETTINGS

    How a raffle runs

    Entry is free and everyone gets exactly one ticket; subs, gifts, Bits and points change nothing. The draw uses your PC's secure random numbers. Winners are always announced in chat.

    PRIZE

    Giveaway tickets for the winners

    —

    Each winner is given the tickets above for the active ticketed giveaway, the same way Dreadnought victories pay. That needs a ticketed giveaway that is collecting and Voyager - Ticket Giveaway v1.5 in Streamer.bot. With none running the raffle still plays, but no tickets are given. Set the prize to 0 to run a raffle for something you hand out yourself.
